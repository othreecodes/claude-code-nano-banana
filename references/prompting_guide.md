# Nano Banana Prompting Guide

## Prompt Formula

```
<action> <subject> <details> <style/setting>
```

**Example**: "Create an image of a golden retriever wearing sunglasses on a beach at sunset"

## Effective Prompting Tips

### Be Specific
❌ "A woman in a dress"
✅ "A young woman in a flowing red dress running through a park at golden hour"

### Include Details
- **Subject**: Who/what is the main focus
- **Action**: What is happening
- **Setting**: Where is this taking place
- **Style**: Photorealistic, cartoon, watercolor, etc.
- **Lighting**: Golden hour, dramatic shadows, soft diffused
- **Mood**: Cheerful, mysterious, serene

### For Text in Images
Nano Banana Pro excels at text rendering:
- Specify exact text in quotes
- Mention font style if important
- Describe placement

**Example**: "Create a poster with the text 'SUMMER SALE' in bold yellow letters on a tropical beach background"

## Editing Prompts

When editing an uploaded image:

### Background Changes
- "Replace the background with a mountain landscape"
- "Blur the background for a portrait effect"

### Object Manipulation
- "Remove the person on the left"
- "Add a coffee cup on the table"

### Style Transfer
- "Apply a watercolor painting style"
- "Make this look like a vintage photograph"

### Adjustments
- "Convert to black and white"
- "Make it night time with city lights"
- "Change her dress from blue to red"

## Character Consistency

To maintain character appearance across multiple images:
1. Start with a clear character description or reference image
2. Use chat/multi-turn for iterations
3. Reference "the same character" in follow-up prompts

## Common Use Cases

### Product Mockups
"Place this product on a white marble countertop with soft studio lighting"

### Infographics
"Create an infographic about climate change showing temperature rise from 1900 to 2024"

### Social Media
"Design an Instagram post announcing a 50% off sale with modern minimalist style"

### Storyboards
"Create a storyboard panel showing a character entering a mysterious forest"
